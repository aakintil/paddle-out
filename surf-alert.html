Estimated, not measured. Built from one real high tide reading (Aug 21, 10:43am) repeated on a standard 12h25m semi‑diurnal cycle. There is no free live tide station covering this stretch of the Lagos coast.
Wave face height
A generic swell height × period model, not measured at these spots.
Distance from shore (close in / mid lineup / the break)
Typical assumptions for a sandy beach break, not measured at Takwa Bay or Lighthouse. Checked against GEBCO global seafloor data: its grid is about 450m across, far too coarse to resolve anything at this scale, so there's currently no reliable way to verify real distances here.
