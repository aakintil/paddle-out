# Surf condition illustration prompts

Shared direction: square editorial surf-condition artwork; handmade four-to-five-color silkscreen; warm cream paper; vibrant tropical turquoise and cobalt-teal inks; near-black figures; tangerine-coral accents; large simplified shapes; restrained dry-brush texture; full bleed; no typography, border, logo, gradients, photography, or 3D rendering.

## GO NOW — Big swell, clean

A Black surfer crouches low on the lower-middle open face, immediately ahead of the breaking pocket on the right. The rider looks left down the line; the coral board points diagonally down-left with its tail higher and nearer the breaking section.

## GO NOW — Dawn patrol

A tangerine sun just crests a straight horizon above calm turquoise water, with a broken path of reflected light and subtle clean swell lines; spacious, warm, and expectant; no figure.

## WORTH PLANNING — Small but rideable

A relaxed Black surfer seen from behind wades through waist-high water toward two small clean swell lines, carrying exactly one fully visible coral longboard horizontally across the waist.

## TOO SMALL — Swell present, nothing to ride

A Black surfer sits patiently in calm turquoise water, seen from behind on one horizontal coral board. Several tiny parallel swell lines gently lift but never crest or break; the sea is alive, just not rideable.

## TOO BIG — Oversized swell

One enormous clean ocean swell towers over a tiny Black surfer paused safely at the shoreline, holding one coral surfboard horizontally under an arm. The sea is beautiful and alluring rather than stormy, but the dramatic scale difference clearly says it is too large to paddle out here.

## GO TO LIGHTHOUSE INSTEAD — Better at the exposed point

A Black surfer walks purposefully along a diagonal shoreline with one horizontal coral board, leaving weak messy water behind and heading toward a distant dark headland. A small clean turquoise wave peels beside a minimal lighthouse silhouette, making the alternate destination legible without text or arrows.

## MARGINAL — Wind chop

An abstract edge-to-edge field of irregular short wave facets colliding in conflicting directions, using turquoise, cobalt, blue-gray, and cream; restless but not stormy; no figure or horizon.

## SKIP — Flat

One long surfboard lies alone on pale sand below a thin band of perfectly flat muted water; high horizon, vast negative space, quiet and gently disappointing; no person or beach objects.

## SKIP — Stormy

Heavy diagonal blue-gray rain crosses near-black sky and dark rough water, separated by one thin cream horizon; two low wave forms, graphic and severe; no figure, board, boat, or lightning.
